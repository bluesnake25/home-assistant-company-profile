Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10/1/2025

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when using our integration with Home Assistant.

  1. Information We Collect

    We do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or payment data.

    The integration only processes technical data required for the operation of your devices (e.g., status, sensor values).

  2. How We Use Information

    Data is processed locally in your Home Assistant installation.

    We do not store, sell, or share your data with third parties.

  3. Data Sharing

    No data is transmitted to us or to any third-party service.

    All data remains under your control within your Home Assistant environment.

  4. Security

    We recommend that you secure your Home Assistant installation and DuckDNS domain with HTTPS and a strong password.
